# Annotation Guidelines

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the annotation guidelines for classifying legal acts into the project's shared legal taxonomy.

The purpose is to ensure consistent classification across all team members and datasets.

## 2. Shared Legal Taxonomy

The project uses the following 10 legal areas:

1. Constitutional Law
2. Criminal Law
3. Civil Law
4. Public Administration
5. Taxation and Finance
6. Employment and Social Protection
7. Healthcare
8. Education and Culture
9. Economy and Business
10. Environment and Infrastructure

## 3. General Annotation Rules

- Classify documents according to their primary subject matter.
- Consider the main purpose and legal provisions of the document.
- Do not classify a document based only on isolated keywords.
- Use the same taxonomy consistently across all datasets.
- If a document covers multiple areas, identify its dominant legal area.
- If the dominant area cannot be determined reliably, mark the document as ambiguous.

## 4. Annotation Guidelines by Legal Area

### 4.1 Constitutional Law

**Definition:**

Legal acts concerning the fundamental principles of the state, constitutional structure, organization of government, and fundamental rights and freedoms.

**Include documents related to:**
- Constitutional provisions and amendments.
- Organization and powers of state institutions.
- Fundamental human rights and freedoms.
- Elections and constitutional procedures.
- Parliamentary procedures and constitutional responsibilities.

**Exclude documents related to:**
- Ordinary criminal or civil proceedings.
- General administrative procedures without constitutional relevance.
- Regular financial or economic regulations.

**Examples:**
- Amendments to the Constitution.
- Laws defining the powers of Parliament.
- Laws regulating constitutional rights.


### 4.2 Criminal Law

**Definition:**

Legal acts concerning criminal offences, criminal responsibility, penalties, and the procedures for investigating and prosecuting criminal activities.

**Include documents related to:**
- Definition and classification of criminal offences.
- Criminal liability and responsibility.
- Criminal penalties and sanctions.
- Criminal investigations and prosecution.
- Rules governing criminal proceedings.

**Exclude documents related to:**
- Civil disputes and compensation claims.
- General administrative violations.
- Employment disputes that do not involve criminal offences.

**Examples:**
- Criminal Code.
- Laws regulating criminal procedure.
- Laws defining penalties for criminal offences.


### 4.3 Civil Law

**Definition:**

Legal acts regulating relationships between individuals and legal entities, including personal rights, property, contracts, obligations, and civil disputes.

**Include documents related to:**
- Contracts and contractual obligations.
- Property ownership and property rights.
- Family and inheritance law.
- Civil liability and compensation.
- Personal rights and legal relationships between individuals.

**Exclude documents related to:**
- Criminal offences and criminal penalties.
- Public administration and government organization.
- Commercial regulations primarily concerning business operations.

**Examples:**
- Laws on obligations.
- Property ownership laws.
- Family law regulations.
- Inheritance law.


### 4.4 Public Administration

**Definition:**

Legal acts regulating the organization, responsibilities, and functioning of public authorities and administrative institutions.

**Include documents related to:**
- Organization of government ministries and public institutions.
- Administrative procedures and public services.
- Responsibilities and powers of administrative authorities.
- Public sector management.
- Civil service and public administration employees.

**Exclude documents related to:**
- Constitutional structure and fundamental state principles.
- Private relationships between individuals.
- Criminal proceedings and penalties.

**Examples:**
- Laws on public administration.
- Laws on civil service.
- Regulations governing administrative procedures.


### 4.5 Taxation and Finance

**Definition:**

Legal acts regulating taxation, public revenue, government budgets, financial institutions, and the management of public funds.

**Include documents related to:**
- Income taxes, corporate taxes, and value-added tax (VAT).
- Tax collection and tax administration.
- Government budgets and public expenditure.
- Public debt and financial management.
- Financial institutions and public finance.

**Exclude documents related to:**
- General business activities without a financial or taxation focus.
- Employment regulations concerning wages and working conditions.
- Administrative procedures unrelated to financial matters.

**Examples:**
- Income Tax Law.
- Value Added Tax Law.
- Laws on government budgets.
- Public finance regulations.

### 4.6 Employment and Social Protection

**Definition:**

Legal acts regulating employment relationships, workers' rights, working conditions, social security, and social welfare systems.

**Include documents related to:**
- Employment contracts and termination of employment.
- Working hours, salaries, and paid leave.
- Workers' rights and occupational safety.
- Pensions and retirement benefits.
- Social assistance and social security.
- Unemployment benefits and protection.

**Exclude documents related to:**
- General business regulations unrelated to employment.
- Tax regulations unrelated to employment benefits.
- Healthcare services and medical treatment.

**Examples:**
- Labour Law.
- Pension and Disability Insurance Law.
- Laws on social protection.
- Occupational Health and Safety regulations.


### 4.7 Healthcare

**Definition:**

Legal acts regulating healthcare systems, medical services, public health, patient rights, and the organization of healthcare institutions.

**Include documents related to:**
- Organization and financing of healthcare services.
- Patient rights and responsibilities.
- Medical professionals and healthcare institutions.
- Public health protection and disease prevention.
- Medicines, medical devices, and pharmaceutical regulations.
- Health insurance and access to healthcare.

**Exclude documents related to:**
- General social assistance unrelated to healthcare.
- Employment regulations for workers in general.
- Environmental protection without a direct public health focus.

**Examples:**
- Law on Health Care.
- Law on Health Insurance.
- Laws regulating medicines and medical devices.
- Public Health Protection regulations.


### 4.8 Education and Culture

**Definition:**

Legal acts regulating educational systems, schools, universities, cultural institutions, and the preservation and promotion of cultural activities.

**Include documents related to:**
- Primary, secondary, and higher education.
- Organization and accreditation of educational institutions.
- Teachers, students, and educational standards.
- Cultural institutions, museums, and libraries.
- Protection of cultural heritage.
- Arts and cultural activities.

**Exclude documents related to:**
- General employment regulations unrelated to education.
- Healthcare education without broader educational relevance.
- General business activities involving educational services.

**Examples:**
- Law on Primary Education.
- Law on Higher Education.
- Laws on Cultural Heritage.
- Regulations governing educational institutions.


### 4.9 Economy and Business

**Definition:**

Legal acts regulating economic activities, commercial operations, businesses, trade, competition, and the organization of markets.

**Include documents related to:**
- Business establishment and company operations.
- Commercial contracts and business activities.
- Domestic and international trade.
- Market competition and consumer protection.
- Industry, production, and economic development.
- Business registration and corporate governance.

**Exclude documents related to:**
- General taxation and public budgeting.
- Employment rights and social protection.
- Property and personal relationships unrelated to business activities.

**Examples:**
- Company Law.
- Competition Law.
- Consumer Protection Law.
- Trade and Commerce regulations.


### 4.10 Environment and Infrastructure

**Definition:**

Legal acts regulating environmental protection, natural resources, energy, transportation, construction, and public infrastructure.

**Include documents related to:**
- Environmental protection and pollution control.
- Climate change and conservation of natural resources.
- Waste management and water protection.
- Energy production and distribution.
- Roads, railways, and other transportation systems.
- Construction, urban planning, and public infrastructure.

**Exclude documents related to:**
- Healthcare regulations without a direct environmental focus.
- General business activities unrelated to infrastructure or environmental matters.
- Agricultural business activities without an environmental focus.

**Examples:**
- Environmental Protection Law.
- Waste Management Law.
- Energy Law.
- Road Traffic and Infrastructure regulations.
- Construction and Spatial Planning regulations.


## 5. Handling Ambiguous and Multi-Area Documents

Some legal acts may contain provisions related to multiple legal areas.

The following rules should be applied:

1. Assign the legal area that best represents the document's primary purpose.
2. Do not assign a category based only on a minor provision or a single keyword.
3. If two or more areas are equally relevant, mark the document as ambiguous.
4. If the document does not clearly fit any of the ten categories, mark it as unclassified.
5. When uncertain, team members should discuss the document and agree on the most appropriate category.

## 6. Annotation Consistency

- All team members must use the same ten-category taxonomy.
- The same classification criteria must be applied to all datasets.
- Uncertain cases should be documented and reviewed.
- Any changes to annotation rules must be agreed upon by the team.
